# Certification Practice Questions

This context describes the questions and answers used to prepare for the Claude certification architect exam.

## Language

**Practice question**:
An English multiple-choice question used for exam preparation, consisting of a question and its answer choices.

**Answer choice**:
One of the possible answers presented with a **practice question**. Its position identifies it within that question.

**Selected answer**:
An **answer choice** identified as correct for a **practice question**. A question may have one or more selected answers.

## Example dialogue

**Learner:** What is the selected answer to this practice question?

**Developer:** This question asks for two answers: the second and fourth choices, shown as B and D with their text.
